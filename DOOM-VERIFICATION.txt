DOOM verification
================

DOOM is included in the USB image:
- doom.pico
- doom1.wad

The payload names were verified in PicoOS-Pro-2.1-usb-small.img.
PicoOS-USB.img was rebuilt from the same DOOM-enabled USB image.

The 1.44 MB legacy BIOS floppy image cannot contain the 4.2 MB DOOM WAD.
The supported way to run DOOM is the 9 MB USB image through its UEFI path.
In that environment the app archive is loaded by PicoOS and DOOM appears in Start.
